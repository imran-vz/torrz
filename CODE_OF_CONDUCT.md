# Code of conduct

Torrz follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not
the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer,
[@imran-vz](https://github.com/imran-vz), through GitHub. Reports are read in confidence. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code.
